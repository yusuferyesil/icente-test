Conference Format

100% Virtual / Online Conference

Global Access via Virtual Platform

ICENTE 2026 will be held entirely online. Join us from anywhere in the world!

In order to foster a truly global exchange of knowledge and to accommodate the busy schedules of researchers worldwide, ICENTE 2026 will be organized as a 100% Remote / Virtual Conference. There will be no physical venue for this year's event.

Live Presentations

All keynote speeches, technical sessions, and paper presentations will be conducted via live video conferencing platforms (e.g., Zoom/Microsoft Teams). Links will be provided to registered attendees.

Digital Certificates

Authors who successfully present their papers in the virtual sessions will receive their official digital Certificates of Presentation and Attendance shortly after the conference.

Technical Requirements

Presenters are kindly requested to ensure they have a stable internet connection, a working microphone, and a webcam. Detailed presentation guidelines and virtual meeting links will be shared with registered participants closer to the conference date.
