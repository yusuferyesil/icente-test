Registration Information

An author can have their name on a maximum of two abstracts/papers/posters in the conference. One registration fee includes only one abstract/paper/poster presentation. Additional Paper: 50% discount will be applied for the second article. Registration is mandatory to attend the conference. At least one of the authors of the abstract/paper/poster should register and attend the conference.
