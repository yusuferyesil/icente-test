Call for Papers

ICENTE 2026 invites researchers and professionals to submit original papers describing significant advances in engineering technologies. All submissions will be peer-reviewed and selected based on originality, relevance, and technical soundness.

Submission Guidelines

Applications for ICENTE 2026 Conference are received through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT). Before submission, please use the appropriate template:

  • Full-Text Submissions: Use the full-paper template.
  • Abstract Submissions: Use the abstract template.

Upload your work, prepared according to the relevant template, via the conference application system. Kindly include the iThenticate report when submitting the full text. All submissions will undergo double-blind peer review by experts in the respective fields.

Publication

The papers submitted will be evaluated by the Scientific Committee, and the papers approved by the scientific committee will be presented at the conference. All of the papers accepted and presented at the conference will be published in the abstracts book. Full-text papers will be published by one of the following options.

International Journal of Automotive Engineering and Technologies (IJAET)
Peer-reviewed Journal
Selcuk University Journal of Engineering Sciences (SUJES)
Peer-reviewed Journal

Electrical Engineering and Energy
Peer-reviewed Journal
New Trends in Computer Sciences
Peer-reviewed Journal
Turkish Journal of Mathematics and Computer Science
Peer-reviewed Journal
International Journal of Aeronautics and Astronautics (IJAA)
Peer-reviewed Journal

Note: Acceptance to the conference does not guarantee publication in the listed journals. Selected papers will be subject to further peer review.

Full-text articles can be published as book chapters if the author prefers and the scientific committee approves. International Book Chapters meet the appointment criteria and academic incentive criteria. In order for book chapters to be eligible for associate professorship criteria, there must be enough applications from the applied engineering fields. The book will be published as an e-book (ISBN) by an international publishing house with one or more editors. Please note that all papers selected for publication will be subject to a peer review process. This does not mean that all selected papers will be accepted for publication in the previously mentioned journals. We hope this conference will be of interest to you. Abstracts will be collected in a book and published on the conference website. For participants who do not wish to submit their full-text papers to journals or have them published as book chapters, the full-text papers will be published online in a proceedings book on the conference website.
